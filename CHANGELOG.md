# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Put human attention first in `STATUS.md`: a numbered **Needs you** section directly under the goal lists every open decision, approval or human-only action, including questions asked in chat and still unanswered, each with a recommendation and what waits on it. Answered items leave the brief and are recorded in their issue; `Needs you: nothing.` when none.

- Keep `STATUS.md` in place until a human reviews and acknowledges the disposition of the whole sprint or assignment. A timer expiring, a lane closing or an agent handoff is not approval to remove it; show actual paused, blocked or review-pending states.

- Keep the explicit goal, done condition and short outcome bullets when adding a compact issue-status table. Report waiting, staged and active work accurately, and keep sprint updates out of the durable queue.

- Keep the full approved sprint or batch visible in the temporary goal brief. A blocker narrows the current step, not the goal; distinguish delivered, independent and queued outcomes, and continue unblocked authorized work without creating busywork.

- Make direct single-agent delivery the default; explicitly configured teams and independent review remain optional unless repository/user policy requires them. Preserve bounded discovery, checkpoint review, useful test evidence and verified local delivery.
- Refine ignored `STATUS.md` into a temporary goal brief for sustained approved work, not a permanent dashboard: goal, observable done condition, 3–5 outcomes with one NOW, normally 100–150 words, one timestamp and only actionable blockers/choices. No brief for quick actions/discussion; retire on completion/cancellation/long-term deferral. Keep one publisher, durable decisions elsewhere and quiet batched updates; no authority or extra work comes from the brief.
- Preserve isolated remote issue reservation/local handoff as a conditional repository policy, not a prerequisite for local/offline projects. Keep queue/issue records authoritative and populated briefings out of git.
